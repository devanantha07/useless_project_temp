### Project Description
A humorous webcam-based facial-expression challenge game inspired by the exaggerated comic expressions of Malayalam cinema. The game uses real AI facial landmark detection (MediaPipe) to analyze your face and score your expression against a target. Show the expression. Unlock the level. 

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
People sit with deadpan, emotionless faces in front of their computers all day. There is a severe lack of dramatic, exaggerated Malayalam cinema expressions in our daily tech interactions.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
A 100% useless, 0% practical, yet totally over-engineered web game that forces you to make ridiculous faces at your webcam. It uses 478 points of real-time AI facial landmark detection to judge your expressions (from "Suspicious" to "Ultimate Drama") and scores you based on blendshape analysis.
